Our Story

From Passion to Production

KINETIX was born from a single belief: every brand has a powerful story waiting to be told through motion. What began as a small team of passionate filmmakers in Burewala has grown into Pakistan's most trusted motion design studio.

We combine cinematic storytelling, precision 3D/CGI artistry, and strategic brand thinking to craft videos that don't just look beautiful — they convert, retain, and inspire loyal audiences.

Every frame is crafted with the obsessive attention of a film production house and the strategic clarity of a world-class creative agency.

How We Work

Our Production Pipeline

A battle-tested 4-phase process refined over 8 years and 350+ successful productions.

PHASE 01

Discovery & Strategy

Deep-dive brand session to understand your goals, audience, competitors, and the story only your brand can tell. We map every outcome before touching a single frame.

PHASE 02

Script & Storyboard

Professional copywriters craft your script. Our illustrators produce detailed storyboards and animatics so you can visualize the final product before production begins.

PHASE 03

Motion Production

Our elite team of 3D artists, motion designers, and VFX specialists bring your storyboard to life in stunning 4K 60FPS with milestone reviews throughout.

PHASE 04

Delivery & Support

Final deliverables in all required formats — social, broadcast, web. Plus 30 days of post-launch support to ensure your content performs exactly as planned.
